## What the company is

Cadence is an observability platform for API-driven engineering teams. It
catches reliability and latency regressions before customers feel them, and
replaces a patchwork of dashboards, log greps, and on-call guesswork. Its
output is what platform leaders use to decide where to invest engineering
time and whether a release is safe to ship.

---

## GTM phases (drives Strategic Fit)

- **Design Partner** — through Q2 — hand-picked teams, deep feedback, no
  self-serve. Awareness matters more than volume.
- **Self-Serve Beta** — Q3–Q4 — open signups, product-led pipeline. This is
  the conversion window.
- **GA** — next year — full launch, land-and-expand.

We are currently in **Design Partner** phase heading into Beta. Events that
build awareness just before the Beta window (late Q2), or drive signups
during it (Q3–Q4), score highest.

## ICP personas (all equally ICP unless noted)

- **Builders** — platform engineers, SREs, backend engineers, DevOps.
- **Decision influencers** — staff/principal engineers, engineering
  managers, tech leads who set tooling standards.
- **Economic buyers** — VP Engineering, CTO, Director of Platform /
  Infrastructure at scaling companies.

**Persona-scoring notes:**

- We have **no capital-stakeholder or investor persona** — this is a
  bottoms-up, product-led motion. Do not count investors, analysts, or
  press as ICP.
- Staff and principal engineers are ICP even without a management title:
  they are the standards-setters who bring tools in.
- Do NOT score a developer event low just because the crowd is "individual
  engineers" rather than "buyers." In our motion the builder is the entry
  point and the influencer; the VP signs later.

## Team capacity

- Team size: ~18. No dedicated event/field-marketing staff.
- Max 2–3 events per month before pulling engineers off product work.
- Every event has real opportunity cost — engineers who staff a booth
  aren't shipping.

---

## Budget philosophy

- Confirmed paid sponsorships this year: **KubeCon NA** (flagship,
  confirmed) and one workshop slot at **PlatformCon** (negotiating).
- Default posture: **speaking-first, attend-second.**
- Booth-only sponsorships are generally poor ROI at this stage.
- Workshop and talk slots are the high-value piece of any package. When
  negotiating, strip the booth and extra passes to cut cost; keep the stage
  or workshop time.

## Geography
